How training here works

The Chakra Cycle

Every session moves through the same four stages — the wheel the gym is named for. Nothing skipped, nothing rushed.

01

Assess

Coach checks form, mobility, and where you're starting from — every session, not just the first one.

02

Build strength

Free weights and machines, programmed around your current level and goals.

03

Train functionally

Kettlebells, medicine balls, battle ropes and resistance bands for balance, coordination, and core strength.

04

Recover

Cooldown and mobility work, so the next session starts from a better place than the last.
